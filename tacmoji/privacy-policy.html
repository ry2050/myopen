This Privacy Policy applies to TacMoji (the “App”), a tic-tac-toe game developed by myopen.app.

2. Data We May Collect

2.1 Data You Provide

  • We do not collect or upload any content you create in the App. All game data and settings stay on your device.
  • Support communications: only if you contact us by email. We receive only the information you choose to share.

2.2 Automatically Collected Technical Data

The App does not collect personal data, analytics data, or device identifiers. No analytics or crash reporting tools are enabled.

2.3 Permission-Based Data

The App does not request or use any system permissions, including camera, photos, microphone, notifications, or location.

If future versions introduce features that require system permissions, we will clearly notify users in the App and update this Privacy Policy accordingly.

3. How We Use Your Data

We do not collect personal data in the App. If you contact us by email, we use your information only to respond to your inquiry.

4. Third-Party Services

The current version of the App contains no in-app purchases and does not use any third-party SDKs, analytics tools, advertising networks, payment processors, or tracking technologies.

If a future version introduces optional in-app purchases (such as a Tip Jar) or other features that rely on third-party services, we will clearly disclose those services and update this Privacy Policy accordingly before the feature is released.

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We do not collect or store personal data. Game preferences are stored locally on your device and can be cleared by deleting the App. If you contacted us by email, you may request deletion of that correspondence at any time.

6. Security

We do not collect or store personal data in the App. If you contact us by email, we use reasonable safeguards to protect those communications. However, no method is 100% secure.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has contacted us and shared personal data, please contact us to request deletion.

8. Your Rights

Because we do not collect personal data in the App, most data access, correction, or deletion rights are not applicable. If you contact us by email, you may request deletion of those communications.

Contact: support@myopen.app

9. International Users

The App is available globally. Because no personal data is collected by the App, no data is transferred internationally.

10. Updates

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted here with the “Last Updated” date. Material changes may be notified in-app when feasible.
