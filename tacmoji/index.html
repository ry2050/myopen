TacMoji

The classic pencil-and-paper game, reimagined. Four board sizes, smart AI, two-player face-off, and pieces that are anything you want.

Free forever. No ads. No tracking. Open source.

Features

Four Boards

  • 3×3 — The classic you know by heart
  • 5×5 — More space, more tactics
  • 7×7 — Epic match-ups that test your true skill
  • 9×9 — The ultimate challenge for true masters
  • Win condition adjusts automatically per board size

Two Ways to Play

  • vs CPU — real Minimax strategy with alpha-beta pruning
  • 2 Players — face off on the same device, no passing needed
  • Opponent's pieces and labels auto-rotate 180° so both sides read clearly
  • No second screen, no account, no setup

Your Pieces, Your Style

  • Any emoji 🐱🚀⭐🦊🎸🍕 — play as literally anything you love
  • Classic shapes ●■▲★ — clean and bold
  • Any letter A–Z — go with your initials
  • Each player picks their own symbol and color independently

Honest to the Core

  • 100% free — no trial periods, no locked features
  • Zero ads — not now, not ever
  • Open source — every line of code on GitHub
  • 8 languages — EN, 繁中, 简中, 한국어, 日本語, DE, FR, ES
  • Light & Dark mode that follows your system
