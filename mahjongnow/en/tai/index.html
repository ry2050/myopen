Scoring

Taiwanese mahjong scoring

In Taiwanese mahjong, a winning hand is scored in tai. You add up every pattern the hand contains: no fu, no doubling. Below is each pattern, what it takes and what it's worth, with an example hand where a picture helps. The Chinese name next to each one is what players at the table will call it.

Tai values match how Taiwan Mahjong Now counts them. House rules differ from table to table (a cap on tai, multiple winners on one discard), so agree on them before you start.

Reading the examples: the tile set apart on the right is the winning tile. Sets with a line underneath were called (chow, pung or kong).

Concealed and self-draw

Concealed

門清 1 tai

Win on a discard without having called any chow, pung or exposed kong.

Self Draw

自摸 1 tai

Win by drawing the winning tile yourself, after calling at least one set.

Concealed Self Draw

門清一摸三 3 tai

Win by self-draw without calling anything. Counted together as 3 tai.

All Melds Single Wait

全求 2 tai

Call all five sets and win on a discard with one tile left in your hand. The single wait's 1 tai isn't added.

All Melds Self-Draw

半求 1 tai

The same shape as All Melds Single Wait, but won by self-draw. Self Draw's 1 tai is added.

Waits

Two-sided waits and waits on either of two pairs add nothing.

Edge Wait

邊張 1 tai

Win on a 3 while holding 1-2, or on a 7 while holding 8-9.

Closed Wait

中洞 1 tai

Win on the middle tile, like a 5 while holding 4 and 6.

Single Wait

單吊 1 tai

Win waiting on one tile to finish your pair.

Hand shapes

All Sequences

平胡 2 tai

All five sets are sequences, the pair is a suit tile, you have no flowers, and you win on a two-sided wait.

All Pungs

碰碰胡 4 tai

All five sets are pungs (kongs count).

Winds and dragons

Round Wind

圈風刻 1 tai

A pung of the round wind.

Seat Wind

門風刻 1 tai

A pung of your seat wind. If it's also the round wind, both count.

Dragon Pung

中刻・發刻・白刻 1 tai

A pung of red, green or white dragons. 1 tai for each.

Little 3 Dragons

小三元 4 tai

Pungs of two dragons, with the third dragon as your pair. The dragon pungs aren't counted separately.

Big 3 Dragons

大三元 8 tai

Pungs of all three dragons. The dragon pungs aren't counted separately.

Little 4 Winds

小四喜 8 tai

Pungs of three winds, with the fourth wind as your pair. Round and seat wind pungs aren't counted separately.

Big 4 Winds

大四喜 16 tai

Pungs of all four winds. Round and seat wind pungs aren't counted separately.

Concealed pungs

3 Concealed Pungs

三暗刻 2 tai

Three pungs you didn't call (concealed kongs count). If you win on a discard, the pung that tile completes doesn't count.

4 Concealed Pungs

四暗刻 5 tai

Four pungs you didn't call.

5 Concealed Pungs

五暗刻 8 tai

Five pungs you didn't call. All Pungs' 4 tai always comes with it.

One suit

Half Flush

混一色 4 tai

One suit plus honor tiles.

Full Flush

清一色 8 tai

One suit only, with no honor tiles.

All Honors

字一色 8 tai

Honor tiles only: winds and dragons.

Flowers

Matching Flower

正花 1 tai

1 tai for each flower whose number matches your seat. East is 1 (spring, plum), South is 2 (summer, orchid), and so on.

Flower Set

花槓 2 tai

All four seasons, or all four flowers. That set's matching flower isn't counted separately.

8 Immortals

八仙過海 8 tai

Collect all eight flowers and you win on the spot, whatever your hand looks like. Self Draw's 1 tai is added.

Seven Robbing One

七搶一 8 tai

If you hold seven flowers and another player draws the eighth, you win on the spot.

Special wins and ready hands

Last Tile

海底撈月 1 tai

Win by self-draw on the last tile of the wall.

Win on Kong Replacement

槓上開花 1 tai

Win on the replacement tile you draw after a kong.

Robbing Kong

搶槓胡 1 tai

Win on the tile another player is adding to their pung.

Declared Ready

宣告聽牌 1 tai

Declare ready, then win. Once you declare, your hand is locked.

Dealer's First-Discard Ready

天聽 8 tai

The dealer declares ready with their first discard and later wins. Nobody may have called before that, and you can't have passed on a winning tile. The usual 1 tai for declaring isn't added.

First-Discard Ready

地聽 4 tai

A non-dealer declares ready with their first discard and later wins. Same conditions as Dealer's First-Discard Ready.

Heavenly Hand

天胡 24 tai

The dealer wins with the opening hand. No other tai are counted (the dealer tai still is).

Earthly Hand

地胡 16 tai

A non-dealer wins on their first draw. No other tai are counted.

Human Hand

人胡 8 tai

A non-dealer wins on the first tile discarded in the hand.

Dealer and streaks

Dealer

莊家 1 tai

1 tai when the dealer wins, when the dealer deals in, or when another player wins by self-draw. A deal-in between two non-dealers doesn't count it.

Dealer Streak

連N拉N 1+2N tai

If the dealer has kept the deal N times, the dealer's 1 tai becomes 1+2N: 3 tai after one repeat, 5 after two.
