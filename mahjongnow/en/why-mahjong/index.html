Why mahjong

Mahjong is a game night

Four friends, one table, an afternoon. The hands end before the conversation does.

Four reasons

Why people keep coming back to the table

Together at one table

One of the few games that needs four people in the same room. One match is two hours of talking.

Thinking every turn

Tracking tiles, weighing odds, reading what others throw away. A hospital study in Taiwan, reported in 2014, found that older adults who played mahjong regularly for six months improved on a memory test.

Reference: United Daily News health (2014, in Chinese)

A Taiwanese tradition

Sixteen tiles, eight flowers and dealer streaks, played by families every Lunar New Year.

Grandparents and grandkids

Few games let two generations play together and both take it seriously.

One example

Japan calls it “healthy mahjong”

In Japan, mahjong played purely as a social, thinking game has its own name: kenkō mahjong, or healthy mahjong.

Play for the score. No drinking, no smoking.Our own summary of the healthy mahjong idea.

Taiwan Mahjong Now works the same way. It counts tai, and when the match ends you get a report to look back on together.
