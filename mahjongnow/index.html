Features

Taiwan Rules, Done Right

  • Full 16-tile Taiwan Mahjong
  • Complete han scoring, with every hand type recognised
  • Flowers, dealer runs, robbing the kong, kong-on-kong
  • Built for Taiwan rules first — not adapted from another ruleset
  • Pause mid-hand and resume exactly where you left off

Play With Friends

  • Private rooms — a room code is required
  • Never matched with strangers
  • Start with two or three players; AI takes the empty seats
  • Drop out and rejoin your own seat
  • End-of-match report crowns four winners

Your Progress, Tracked

  • Win rate, longest streak, highest-scoring hand
  • Your top three winning hand types
  • Trend over your last 30 games
  • Tracked separately for each AI difficulty
  • Share a hand, a milestone, or your year in review

New to Taiwan Mahjong?

  • Quick Start — playing in five minutes
  • Rule Book — chi, pong, kong, hu and tenpai, one at a time
  • Han Encyclopedia — every hand type and what it scores
  • Practice — questions that explain why you were wrong
  • Three AI difficulties to grow into

Free

Free to play. Nothing to buy.

Every feature above is in the free app — there are no in-app purchases and no subscription. Mahjong Now is supported by advertising: one interstitial appears before the final dealer of each wind round, and never in the middle of a hand. Single-player games work with the network off.
