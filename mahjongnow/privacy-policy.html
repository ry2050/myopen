Privacy Policy for MahJong Now

Last updated: 2026-09-05
Version: 1.1.0

Summary

  • Everything you play against the AI stays on your device. We never upload it.
  • No account is required, and the App has no login.
  • Online play sends a randomly generated ID, the nickname you type, a room code, and your moves to our relay server. The relay keeps them in memory only and never writes them to disk.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob. We serve non-personalized ads and we do not ask for permission to track you.
  • We run no analytics and no crash-reporting service of our own.
  • The game is played for entertainment only. There is no real-money gambling, and scores cannot be exchanged for anything of value.

This summary is for orientation only; the sections below are the operative terms.

1. Data Controller & Contact

Controller: Robert Yang (operating under the brand "MyOpen Studio")
Email: support@myopen.app
Website: https://myopen.app

This Privacy Policy applies to MahJong Now ("the Service" or "the App"), part of the MyOpen app series created under the CodeTo2050 project.

2. Data We Collect

The App has no sign-up and no login. Single-player games, online play, tutorials, and statistics all work without an account, so there is no profile for us to build.

2.1 Data Stored on Your Device

Your game records, statistics, settings, theme, and nickname are stored in a database on your device. We do not upload them. They are removed when you delete the App.

Games played against the built-in AI are entirely local. They involve no network connection of any kind beyond advertising (section 2.4).

2.2 Online Play (Relay Server)

When you create or join an online room, the App connects to a relay server that we operate. The following is transmitted:

  • A player ID — a random identifier (UUID) generated by the App on first use and stored on your device. It is not your device's advertising identifier, and it is not derived from any hardware or account identifier. It exists so that you can reconnect to your own seat after a dropped connection.
  • The nickname you type — shown to the other players in the same room.
  • The room code and your moves during the game.

The relay forwards these between the players in a room and holds them in memory only. It has no database and no attached storage. A room's data ceases to exist when the room ends, and all rooms cease to exist when the server restarts or shuts down — which it does automatically when nobody is playing.

Rooms are also released without anyone having to close them. A room that everybody has left is swept from memory within a few minutes, and a connection that goes quiet is closed automatically shortly after. An abandoned room therefore cannot linger in memory indefinitely.

The relay does not interpret the contents of a game. It forwards messages between players without understanding them.

2.3 IP Address

As with any internet connection, your IP address is visible to the relay server and to our hosting provider while you are connected. We do not store it or use it to build a profile. Our hosting provider may process it for network operation and abuse prevention under its own policy (section 5.3).

2.4 Advertising

The App displays ads supplied by Google AdMob. Google acts as an independent controller for the data it collects and may access your device's advertising identifier and related device and usage data in order to serve and measure ads.

  • We request non-personalized ads for every ad slot in the App.
  • We do not present Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, and we do not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.
  • In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, consent for advertising is collected through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are requested.
  • We do not receive any personal data back from Google. We see only aggregate revenue reporting.

Google's handling of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

2.5 Analytics and Crash Reporting

We use none. The App contains no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK.

2.6 Permission-Based Data

The App does not request access to your camera, photos, microphone, contacts, location, or notifications. If a future version introduces a feature requiring a permission, you will be asked at the time and this Policy will be updated.

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

  • Online play — performance of a contract: we cannot connect you to the other players without transmitting the data in section 2.2.
  • Service operation and abuse prevention — legitimate interest, limited to the short-lived, pseudonymised records described in section 6.
  • Advertising — consent, collected through Google's User Messaging Platform where required.
  • Email support — legitimate interest in answering the question you asked.

4. How We Use Your Data

  • To connect you to the other players in an online room and to let you rejoin your seat after a dropped connection.
  • To keep the service running — capacity planning and fault diagnosis, using the aggregate figures described in section 6.
  • To display ads, as described in section 2.4.

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with data brokers. We do not use it for profiling, and we do not combine it with data from other sources.

5. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

5.1 Apple App Store & Google Play Store (Independent Controllers)

Used for app distribution. They may process device and installation data independently according to their own privacy policies.

5.2 Google AdMob (Independent Controller)

Advertising, as described in section 2.4.

5.3 Fly.io (Processor — Hosting)

Our relay server runs on Fly.io infrastructure in the Tokyo region. Fly.io provides the machines and network on which the relay runs and may process connection metadata, including IP addresses, for network operation.

5.4 No Data Selling

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with third parties for their own advertising or marketing.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

  • On your device — kept until you delete it in the App or uninstall the App. We cannot access it and therefore cannot delete it for you.
  • Room data on the relay — held in memory for the life of the room only. Nothing is written to disk. There is no backup, and no copy survives a restart.
  • Server logs — player IDs and room codes are reduced to short irreversible codes before being written to the operational log, so that a log cannot be used to reconstruct who played with whom. Logs are retained for hours, not days.
  • Operational metrics — whole numbers only, such as how many rooms are open and how long rooms last. They contain no identifiers of any kind. Retained for approximately 30 days.
  • Support email — retained until the matter is resolved; you may ask us to delete it.

7. International Transfers

The relay server is located in Japan. If you play online from outside Japan, the data in section 2.2 is transmitted there for the duration of your session. Given that nothing is stored, the transfer is limited to the live routing of your game.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent age under your local law). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data to us, contact us and we will delete it.

Because the App shows advertising, we request non-personalized ads for all users, which limits the data used to serve them.

9. Security

Connections between the App and the relay server are encrypted in transit. On-device data is protected by your device's own operating-system protections. Because the relay stores nothing, there is no stored database of players to be breached.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

You may request access to, correction of, deletion of, or portability of your personal data, object to processing, or lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority.

In practice, we hold very little that could be returned to you: your game data is on your device and not accessible to us, and relay data does not outlive your session. We will respond to any request and explain precisely what does and does not exist.

There is no account to delete. To remove everything the App holds about you, delete the App.

11. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Material changes may also be shown in‑app when appropriate.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your rights, contact: support@myopen.app
