Features

Three Ways to Play

  • Color matching — bright, high-contrast cards
  • Icon matching — Material symbols across 25+ everyday concepts
  • Photo matching — use your own pictures from the album
  • Six difficulty levels: 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, or 28 cards
  • Customizable themes — long-press any card type to edit

Built for Memory & Learning

  • Smooth 3D flip animation
  • Track completion time and number of moves
  • Per-card pronunciation labels with system Text-to-Speech
  • Square-cropped photos optimized for the card layout
  • Multi-language interface (8 supported languages)
  • Tactile feedback for flip, match, mismatch, and win

Privacy First

  • No account required
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking
  • Photos and settings stay on your device
  • Open source under GPLv3 — audit the code yourself
  • Designed for parents who want safe play for kids
